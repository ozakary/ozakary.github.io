ML-Accelerated Molecular Dynamics and Noble Gas NMR in Porous Materials
Zakary, O.; Lantto, P.; Telkki, V.-V.
EUROMAR 2026  ·  Lodge on Loch Lomond, Scotland  ·  April 2026
Talk
+
Abstract
We present a machine learning-accelerated molecular dynamics framework for simulating xenon diffusion and host–guest dynamics in porous organic cages (CC3) at experimentally relevant timescales. By combining equivariant graph neural network potentials with path-integral techniques, we achieve quantum-mechanical accuracy at a computational cost compatible with microsecond-scale sampling. The resulting trajectories are used to compute ¹²⁹Xe NMR chemical shift distributions via a trained shielding model, enabling direct comparison with variable-temperature NMR experiments. Our results reveal how cage breathing modes, solvent environment, and xenon occupancy jointly determine the observed NMR lineshape, providing a quantitative atomistic picture of guest dynamics in porous liquid hosts.

Fine-Tuning Atomistic Foundation Models for Phase Transition Prediction in Inorganic Phosphides
Zakary, O.; Yin, W.; Aryal, N.; Lantto, P.
SMARTER 9  ·  Torino, Italy  ·  September 2025
Poster
+
Abstract
Foundation models for atomistic simulation have demonstrated remarkable transferability across chemical space, yet their application to complex inorganic phase transitions remains largely unexplored. Here we describe the fine-tuning of MACE-MH-1 on a targeted DFT dataset for RuP, covering both the low-symmetry P2₁/a and the high-symmetry Pnma phases. The fine-tuned model is deployed in extended molecular dynamics runs to characterize the two-stage structural transition near 180 K and 330 K. We discuss the practical requirements for generating informative fine-tuning datasets and the role of symmetry analysis tools (OVITO, spglib) in characterizing intermediate phases from MD trajectories.
